Delivery & FAQ

Everything you need to know about delivery areas, notice times and how enquiries work.

Where we deliver

We deliver across Central Auckland and selected surrounding suburbs:

  • Central Auckland
  • Ponsonby
  • Grey Lynn
  • Parnell
  • Newmarket
  • Mount Eden
  • Epsom
  • Remuera

Delivery to other Auckland locations may be available by arrangement. Delivery charges vary depending on location.

Delivery at a glance

  • Standard delivery Monday to Saturday
  • Allow at least one working day's notice
  • Same-day requests may be possible but are not guaranteed
  • Weddings, events and large corporate orders need extra notice
  • Collection can be arranged

Frequently asked questions

Can I request particular flowers?

Yes. You can request particular flowers or colours, but availability depends on the season and current supply. We may recommend suitable alternatives.

Do you offer same-day delivery?

Same-day delivery may be available for some orders placed early in the day, but it cannot be guaranteed.

Can you accommodate dietary requirements for gift add-ons?

Please include any requirements in your enquiry. Availability of suitable gift items may vary.

How much notice is required for wedding or event flowers?

Please enquire at least three weeks before smaller events, and considerably earlier for weddings or large events.

Do you provide regular flowers for businesses?

Yes. Weekly, fortnightly and other recurring arrangements can be discussed. See our corporate flowers page.

Can I order and pay through the website?

The website is for browsing products and services and sending enquiries. Online payment is not currently available.

Still have a question?

Call us on 09 000 0001 during opening hours, or send an enquiry and we'll get back to you.
